                            Push / Pull / Legs Workout Plan

45 minutes • 6–7 exercises • 4 sets each
Rep scheme for every exercise: 12 / 10 / 10 / 8

Rest periods:
- Compound movements: 75–90 sec
- Isolation movements: 45–60 sec
